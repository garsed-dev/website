Who are we?

Ben Garsed and Daniel Mead are renewable energy specialists delivering high-quality solar and electrical solutions across the commercial and domestic sectors, with a strong focus on complex commercial projects.

We design, install, and support solar PV systems for a wide range of environments, including hospitals, schools, private estates, and large commercial premises.

Alongside our renewable work, we provide a full range of electrical services, including EICRs, rewires, fault finding, and ongoing electrical maintenance.

What sets Sustainable Access apart is our role as a trusted delivery partner for larger businesses. We regularly support contractors and organisations that have secured solar projects but require additional manpower or specialist technical expertise. By integrating seamlessly into existing teams, we help ensure projects are delivered efficiently, safely, and to the highest standard.

If you are struggling to find contractors with the knowledge and experience to help you deliver complex solar projects then please get in touch to work with us.
